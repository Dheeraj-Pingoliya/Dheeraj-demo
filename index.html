About this library

Dragnos-Datasheets is a small, hand-checked index — not a scraper. Every entry links straight to a PDF hosted by the manufacturer (Texas Instruments, Vishay, onsemi, Microchip, Espressif, Arduino, Nexperia, InvenSense/TDK, Murata, Yageo, Songle) or an authorized distributor mirror when the manufacturer doesn't host a direct file. Specs shown on each card are a quick reference only — always confirm exact ratings against the linked datasheet before you design around them.

Datasheets remain the copyright of their respective manufacturers. This page just points to them.
